====================================================
BHAVANI CRAFTS - ADMIN PORTAL USAGE & OPERATIONS GUIDE
====================================================
Version: 1.0 (March 2026)
Architecture: Multi-Tiered Enterprise Control Panel

This document serves as the official master manual for operating the Admin Portal of Bhavani Crafts Online. The portal has been custom-built to offer absolute, centralized control over the B2C (Retail) and B2B (Wholesale Franchise) ecosystems.


===========================
1. TIERED ACCESS PROTOCOLS
===========================
The system operates on an automated role-based access control (RBAC) hierarchy.

[Tier 1] SUPER ADMIN (Owner): Has supreme control. Has exclusive access to the 'Employee Registry' to hire or terminate staff access.
[Tier 2] ADMIN: Can manage products, orders, franchises, and broadcasts, but cannot manage employees. 
[Tier 3] EMPLOYEE: Identical operational access to Admin, restricted from sensitive system configurations or peer management.


===========================
2. THE COMMAND DASHBOARD
===========================
Upon logging in as an admin or employee, you land on the Dashboard.
- Active Broadcast Ticker: If there is an ongoing emergency network broadcast, it will pulsate at the very top of the screen.
- Real-Time Analytics: Four premium data cards dynamically track your Total Seekers (Users), Pending Franchise Approvals, Master Catalog Size, and Total Revenue Registry.
- Quick Jump Hub: Instantly jump into 'Franchise Approvals', 'Category Setup', or 'Product Management'.


===========================
3. EMPLOYEE ACCESS REGISTRY (Super Admin Only)
===========================
Navigation path: Sidebar -> Employee Access -> Staff Registry
Use this module to securely onboard new staff.
- Add New Employee: You provide their Name, Email, Phone, and assign a temporary Password.
- Revocation/Block: If an employee leaves the company, hit the "Block Access" button. This immediately freezes their dashboard without destroying their historical data logs. You can unblock them anytime.


===========================
4. GLOBAL BROADCAST SYSTEM
===========================
Navigation path: Sidebar -> Employee Access -> Global Broadcast
This is your mass-communication emergency radio tower.
- Function: Creates a prominent, unignorable alert banner on user dashboards.
- Audiences: 
   1. 'Staff Only' (Employees only see it).
   2. 'Franchises' (Only Business partners see it on their Hub).
   3. 'Global' (Pushed to absolutely everyone).
- Threat Levels: Set urgency to Information (Blue), Warning (Yellow), or Critical Alert (Pulsing Red).
- Kill Switch: Hit the 'Transmitting' button to instantly pull a broadcast offline without needing to constantly delete and recreate them.


===========================
5. FRANCHISE MASTERY (B2B Approval)
===========================
Navigation path: Sidebar -> Master Actions Hub -> Franchise Mastery
When a new store wants to sell your artifacts, they register as a 'Business'. 
They cannot access the system or wholesale pricing until you approve them.
- Action: Review their submitted Company Name, Tax ID/GST, and intended physical location. Click "Approve Partnership" to unlock their Wholesale Dashboard and generate their B2B Catalog.


===========================
6. PRODUCTS MASTERY & MASTER CATALOG
===========================
Navigation path: Sidebar -> Catalog Silk -> Products Mastery
This is the core inventory engine. 
- Retail vs Wholesale Pricing: Every product takes a standard price (for public customers) and a Wholesale Price (for approved Franchises).
- Stock Engine: Enter precise stock. If a customer or franchise buys it, this central number drops automatically.
- Multi-Tier Variants: When adding Options (like "Size: Large" or "Material: Brass"), you can now add specific Option Descriptions for deeper detail.
- Manual Discounts: You can apply a specific manual discount percentage to any artifact.


===========================
7. SACRED FULFILLMENT (Kanban & List views)
===========================
Navigation path: Sidebar -> Catalog Silk -> Kanban Fulfillment // Order Registry
This module controls exactly how physical artifacts get to buyers.
- List View (Order Registry): Detailed, tabular breakdown of every order, their line items, the customer's contact info, and total financial value. 
- Kanban Board: The operational powerhouse. Four vertical colored lanes (Pending -> Processing -> Shipped -> Delivered). 
- To use Kanban: Literally click, drag, and drop a customer's order card from one column to another. The database instantaneously updates the status silently in the background. No save buttons required.


===========================
8. SECTORS PENDING DEVELOPMENT
===========================
- Headquarters Auto-Restock Approval: Franchises can request bulk restocks via their portal. The HQ approval board is the next scheduled feature to be built into this Admin screen.
- Advanced Accounting / Invoicing module.
