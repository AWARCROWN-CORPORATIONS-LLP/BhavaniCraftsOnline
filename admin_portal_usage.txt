====================================================
BHAVANI CRAFTS - ADMIN PORTAL USAGE & OPERATIONS GUIDE
====================================================
Version: 1.1 (April 2026)
Architecture: Admin Dashboard Control Panel

This document is the manual for the Admin Portal of Bhavani Crafts Online. Use this to manage customers, orders, staff, and products.

===========================
1. USER ACCESS LEVELS
===========================
The system has three types of users:

[Tier 1] SUPER ADMIN (Owner): Full control over everything, including adding or removing staff.
[Tier 2] ADMIN: Can manage products, orders, partners, and announcements but cannot manage staff.
[Tier 3] EMPLOYEE: Same as Admin, but cannot change system settings or manage other staff.

===========================
2. STORE SUMMARY (DASHBOARD)
===========================
When you log in, you see the Store Summary page.
- Announcement Ticker: Any active alerts or news will show at the top of the screen.
- Real-Time Stats: Four cards show Total Customers, New Business Requests, Total Items in store, and Total Sales amount.
- Quick Actions: Shortcuts to manage partners, categories, or products.

===========================
3. STAFF ACCESS (Super Admin Only)
===========================
Navigation: Sidebar -> Staff Access -> Staff Members
Use this to add or manage your team.
- Add New Staff: Enter their Name, Email, Phone, and set a Password.
- Block Access: If someone leaves, click "Block". This stops them from logging in but keeps their history. You can unblock them later.

===========================
4. ANNOUNCEMENTS
===========================
Navigation: Sidebar -> Store Management -> Announcements
Use this to send messages to your users or staff.
- Function: Shows a banner at the top of their screen.
- Audience: You can send to 'Staff Only', 'Partners Only', or 'Everyone'.
- Urgency: Set to Information (Blue), Warning (Yellow), or Critical (Red).
- Switch Off: Use the toggle to hide an announcement without deleting it.

===========================
5. PARTNERS (Branch Registry)
===========================
Navigation: Sidebar -> Store Management -> Partners
When a local shop wants to work with us, they sign up as a 'Business'.
- Action: Check their details (Company Name, GST, Location). Click "Approve" to let them see wholesale prices and start ordering.

===========================
6. PRODUCTS & ITEMS
===========================
Navigation: Sidebar -> Store Items -> Products
This is where you manage what you sell.
- Pricing: Set the regular price and the Wholesale price for partners.
- Stock: Enter how many items you have. The number drops automatically when someone buys.
- 3D Models: You can upload .GLB and .USDZ files to show products in 3D/AR on the website.
- AI Writer: Use the "AI Writer" button to automatically generate product descriptions.

===========================
7. ORDERS (Status Board & List)
===========================
Navigation: Sidebar -> Store Management -> Orders
Manage how orders are shipped and delivered.
- List View: A table showing all orders, customer names, and total amounts.
- Status Board (Kanban): A drag-and-drop board with four columns (Pending -> Processing -> Shipped -> Delivered).
- Using the Board: Click and drag an order card to the next column as you process it. The status updates automatically.

===========================
8. UPCOMING FEATURES
===========================
- Auto-Restock: Letting partners ask for more stock automatically.
- Better Accounting: More detailed financial reports and automated invoices.
